How it works

  1. Get paired

    Each week the circle pairs available members. Odd number? Someone gets a solo practice room.

  2. Agree a time

    Propose times in your own time zone and chat with your partner.

  3. Practise together

    Shared editor, whiteboard, and verified test runs in JavaScript or Python. Swap interviewer and candidate.

Private by default

  • Invite-only. Members are approved by a circle owner.
  • Just you and your partner. Your sessions stay private to the two of you.
  • No noise. No recruiters, no ads, no public profiles.
Privacy note

Randori Circle is a private practice space run by its owners. Members see the names of the people in their circle. Your editor, whiteboard and chat in a pairing room are shared only with your partner for that week.

If you request an invite, the circle owners see your name, email and note so they can decide. A request that isn’t answered expires after 30 days. Once an owner approves or declines it, your name, email and note are removed from the request. If you’re approved, you get a normal invitation email.
