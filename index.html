How Randori works

randori = free practice

You don't pick your partner. Each Sunday the circle publishes one pairing cycle. If the count is odd, one member gets Solo practice — no trios, no one sits out. After pairing, you choose DSA / System Design / Both yourselves. Published pairs do not change during the cycle.
