What Randori is

Randori is a free, invite-only community tool for practising coding interviews. Members of a circle are paired each week, agree a time, and practise together with a shared code editor, whiteboard, chat and a code runner for practice exercises. There is no charge, and nothing is sold through it.

You need an invitation from a circle owner to join. A circle owner may also approve a request to join.
